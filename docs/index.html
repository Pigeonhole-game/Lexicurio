How it works

  • Everyone gets the same seven words each day.
  • Type one synonym per word. Everyday synonyms score about 10 points; eclectic ones score up to 100.
  • Your seven scores add up to a final score, ranked on a daily Game Center leaderboard.
  • The day's top three players win gold, silver and bronze medals.
