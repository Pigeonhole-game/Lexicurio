Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026. Draft: review before publishing.

What Lexicurio stores

Your game history, medals and purchase status are stored on your device. Lexicurio does not run its own servers and does not collect your name, email or contacts.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, your scores and Game Center display name are sent to Apple to show leaderboards and achievements. Apple's privacy policy applies to that data.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location and information about ad interactions to serve and measure ads. Personalized ads are shown only if you allow tracking when iOS asks and, where required by law, give consent in the privacy form. You can change your choice at any time in the app's Settings tab or in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Purchases of Lexicurio Pro are handled by Apple. Lexicurio never sees your payment details.

Children

Lexicurio is not directed at children under 13.
