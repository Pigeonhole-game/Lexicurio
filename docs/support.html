Support

I bought Lexicurio Pro but still see ads

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

My score isn't on the leaderboard

Scores are posted through Game Center. Sign in under iOS Settings > Game Center, then play the next daily game. Only the first game of each day is ranked.

When do medals arrive?

Each day closes at midnight UTC. The next time you open the app, it checks the previous day's final standings and awards your medal if you placed in the top three.

A synonym I typed wasn't accepted

Lexicurio's thesaurus is based on WordNet and is still growing. Let us know which word you expected.
